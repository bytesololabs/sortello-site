Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

ByteSolo Labs builds software that respects your attention, time, and privacy. Sortello is designed from the ground up to operate with zero collection of personal identifiable information.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • We do not require user accounts, social log-ins, or profile creation.
  • We do not track your location or access your device's contacts or photos.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties.

3. Local Storage on Device

All game data—including your current puzzle states, move history, undos, completion dates, streak counters, and player preferences (such as haptic feedback)—is stored strictly on your local device via local preferences storage.

If you reset game data in Settings or clear your browser / app cache, all locally stored puzzle history will be erased.

4. Anonymous Diagnostics & Crash Reporting

To ensure game stability, balance puzzle difficulty, and fix software defects, Sortello may receive anonymous diagnostic telemetry (such as crash stack traces and game level completion events) via standard developer tools.

This diagnostic data is completely aggregated, anonymized, and cannot be linked to any individual user.

5. Deep Links & Shared Puzzles

When you share a puzzle link with a friend (e.g., https://sortello.bytesololabs.com/g?g=...), the link contains only non-personal puzzle generation seeds so the recipient can play the identical board layout. No personal identifiers or device metadata are embedded in shared links.
